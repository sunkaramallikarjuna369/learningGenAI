Tokens & Probabilities

How AI reads text and predicts what comes next

Step 1: Breaking Text into Tokens

What's Happening?

AI doesn't read words like we do. It breaks text into smaller pieces called tokens. Some tokens are whole words, some are word parts, and some are just characters. Type something below to see how it gets tokenized!

Technical Details

Modern LLMs use subword tokenization (BPE, WordPiece, or SentencePiece). This balances vocabulary size with the ability to handle rare words. The tokenizer maps text to integer IDs that index into an embedding matrix.

Step 2: Predicting the Next Token

What's Happening?

After reading your tokens, the AI calculates the probability of every possible next token. It's like a super-powered autocomplete that considers thousands of options. Watch the bars below to see which tokens are most likely!

Technical Details

The model computes P(x_n | x_1, ..., x_{n-1}) via a forward pass through transformer layers, projecting to vocabulary size, then applying softmax to get a probability distribution over ~50,000+ tokens.

Step 3: Temperature - Controlling Creativity

What's Happening?

Temperature controls how "creative" or "random" the AI is. Low temperature = predictable and safe. High temperature = creative but potentially weird. Try the slider below!

Technical Details

Temperature T scales logits before softmax: P(x) = softmax(z/T). T<1 sharpens the distribution (more deterministic), T>1 flattens it (more random). T→0 approaches greedy decoding, T→∞ approaches uniform sampling.
