What's Happening?

Press play to see how Traditional AI and Generative AI approach the same problem differently. Traditional AI looks at data and makes decisions (like sorting emails into spam or not spam). Generative AI takes your request and creates something new (like writing a reply to an email).

Technical Details

Traditional AI models learn P(Y|X) - the probability of label Y given input X. They're discriminative models that find decision boundaries. Generative AI models learn P(X) - the full data distribution - allowing them to sample new instances that resemble the training data.

Key Takeaways

🔍

Traditional AI

  • Analyzes existing data
  • Makes predictions & classifications
  • Outputs decisions or labels
  • Learns P(Y|X) - discriminative
✨

Generative AI

  • Creates new content
  • Generates text, images, code
  • Outputs novel creations
  • Learns P(X) - generative
