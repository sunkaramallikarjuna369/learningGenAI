Step 1: Word Embeddings - GPS for Meaning

What's Happening?

Words are converted into lists of numbers (vectors) that capture their meaning. Similar words end up close together in this "meaning space." Try clicking on words to see their positions!

Technical Details

Word embeddings map tokens to dense vectors in R^d. Models like Word2Vec learn these by predicting context words. The resulting space exhibits linear relationships: v(king) - v(man) + v(woman) ≈ v(queen). Cosine similarity measures semantic relatedness.

Step 2: Word Math - Adding and Subtracting Meaning

What's Happening?

The coolest thing about embeddings: you can do math with words! "King - Man + Woman = Queen" actually works because the vectors capture relationships. Try different combinations below!

Technical Details

Vector arithmetic in embedding space: v(king) - v(man) + v(woman) ≈ v(queen). This works because embeddings encode semantic relationships as directions. The "gender" direction is approximately v(woman) - v(man), which can be added to transform words.

Step 3: Autoencoder - Compress and Reconstruct

What's Happening?

An autoencoder learns to squeeze data through a tiny "bottleneck" and then expand it back. It's like summarizing a book in 3 words, then recreating the book from those words. Watch how the image gets compressed and reconstructed!

Technical Details

Autoencoders consist of an encoder z = f(x) and decoder x̂ = g(z). The bottleneck z is the latent representation. Training minimizes reconstruction loss ||x - x̂||². VAEs add a probabilistic twist: z ~ N(μ(x), σ(x)), enabling generation by sampling from the latent space.

Step 4: Exploring the Latent Space

What's Happening?

The "latent space" is where compressed representations live. Moving around in this space creates smooth transitions between different outputs. It's like a map where nearby points create similar images!

Technical Details

VAEs regularize the latent space to be continuous and smooth via KL divergence. This enables interpolation: z_interp = α*z1 + (1-α)*z2 produces meaningful intermediate outputs. Latent diffusion models operate in this compressed space for efficient generation.
