Step 3: Current Limitations

What's Happening?

Despite impressive capabilities, AI has real limitations. Understanding these helps set realistic expectations and use AI more effectively.

Technical Details

Fundamental challenges: hallucination (lack of grounding), reasoning failures (correlation vs causation), distribution shift, alignment difficulties, and computational costs. Active research addresses each area.
