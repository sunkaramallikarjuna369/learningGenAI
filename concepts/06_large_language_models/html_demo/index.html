Step 1: What Makes LLMs "Large"?

What's Happening?

LLMs are called "large" because of their massive scale: billions of parameters (adjustable numbers), trained on trillions of words. Compare different models below to see how they've grown over time!

Technical Details

Parameters are the weights in the neural network. More parameters = more capacity to learn patterns. Training data size and compute also scale. Scaling laws show predictable performance improvements: L(N,D) ∝ N^(-α) + D^(-β).

Step 2: How LLMs Generate Text

What's Happening?

LLMs generate text one word at a time by predicting "what comes next." Each new word is added to the context, and the process repeats. Watch the generation happen token by token!

Technical Details

Autoregressive generation: P(x₁,...,xₙ) = ∏P(xᵢ|x₁,...,xᵢ₋₁). Each forward pass produces logits over vocabulary, softmax gives probabilities, then sampling (greedy, top-k, top-p, or temperature-scaled) selects the next token.

Step 3: How LLMs Are Trained

What's Happening?

LLMs go through multiple training phases: first they learn from massive text data (pre-training), then they learn to follow instructions (fine-tuning), and finally they learn human preferences (RLHF). Watch the training pipeline!

Technical Details

Pre-training: Causal LM objective on web-scale data. SFT: Supervised learning on instruction-response pairs. RLHF: Train reward model on preferences, then optimize policy with PPO. Constitutional AI adds self-critique loops.

Step 4: Capabilities & Limitations

What's Happening?

LLMs are incredibly capable but have important limitations. Understanding both helps you use them effectively. Explore the capabilities and limitations below!

Technical Details

Capabilities emerge from scale (in-context learning, chain-of-thought). Limitations include: finite context window, no real-time knowledge, hallucinations from probabilistic generation, and biases from training data distribution.
